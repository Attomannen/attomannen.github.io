SlatePaint

3D texture painting and baking for iPad and Apple Pencil.

Import a mesh, bake its maps, paint base color, roughness, metallic, height and more on the model, and export textures for Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot or glTF. Layers, fill layers, masks with generators, smart materials and ray-traced baking, worked with touch and Apple Pencil.

On the App Store 26 January 2027

What it does

  • Paint on the modelPressure, tilt and barrel roll on every brush. Paint in 3D, on the flat texture or both side by side, with symmetry. Hold at the end of a stroke to turn it into a clean shape.
  • Layers, masks and generatorsPaint, fill, folder and filter layers. Masks stack effects. Generators read your baked maps for edge wear, dirt and cavities, and Auto level fits each one to your model.
  • Smart materials and scans71 smart materials in PBR, hand-painted and stylized looks, and 53 scanned materials laid on at their real size.
  • Baking on the iPadRay-traced normal, ambient occlusion, curvature, thickness, position, height and ID maps from a high poly, with an automatic cage. Or import maps baked elsewhere.
  • Export for your enginePresets for Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot and glTF, your own presets with channel packing, DDS, EXR and more, and a layered PSD per channel.

Price

Free to download. Importing your own meshes, baking, painting and saving are free. Until you unlock exports, exported textures carry a faint “Made with SlatePaint” pattern, so you can check them in your engine first. One in-app purchase removes the pattern and adds mesh export: $9.99 for the first two weeks after launch, then $19.99. No subscription, no account.

Requirements

iPadOS 26 or later on an iPad with the A14 chip or later: iPad Air (4th generation) and later, iPad (10th generation) and later, iPad mini (6th generation) and later, or any iPad with an M-series chip. An M1 or later is recommended. Apple Pencil is optional but recommended; squeezing an Apple Pencil Pro opens the quick menu.

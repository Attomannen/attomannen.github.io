Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

SlatePaint doesn't collect any data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your work never leaves your iPad unless you export or share it.

In detail

  • Your work stays with youProjects, meshes and textures are kept on your iPad, in SlatePaint's folder in the Files app, and go only where you export or share them.
  • No accounts, analytics or adsSlatePaint has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything over the internet.
  • PurchasesThe unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; SlatePaint only learns whether the unlock was bought.
  • PhotosSlatePaint asks to add to your photo library only when you choose Save Image for a render. It never reads your library.
  • Crash reportsIf you share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may pass anonymous crash and performance reports to the developer.

SlatePaint

Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

SlatePaint doesn't collect any data.

Your work stays with you

Projects, meshes and textures are kept on your iPad, in SlatePaint's folder in the Files app, and go only where you export or share them.

No accounts, analytics or ads

SlatePaint has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything over the internet.

Purchases

The unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; SlatePaint only learns whether the unlock was bought.

Photos

SlatePaint asks to add to your photo library only when you choose Save Image for a render. It never reads your library.

Crash reports

If you share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may pass anonymous crash and performance reports to the developer.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.

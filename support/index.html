Common questions

Where are my projects?

In the Files app, under On My iPad › SlatePaint › Projects. Your work saves by itself every two minutes and when you leave the app.

What does the unlock include?

Importing your own meshes, and exporting textures and meshes. Painting, baking and the sample props are free.

I bought the unlock on another iPad

Open Settings › Purchase › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I use maps baked in another app?

Yes. File › Import mesh maps takes several at once and matches them by name, or import one at a time from its row in Texture Set › Mesh maps.

Where are the gestures and keyboard shortcuts?

Help, the question mark at the top right, lists them all.

Requirements

An iPad with the A14 chip or later, on iPadOS 17 or later: iPad Air (4th generation) and later, iPad (10th generation) and later, iPad mini (6th generation) and later, or any iPad with an M-series chip.

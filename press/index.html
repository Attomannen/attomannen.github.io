Short description

SlatePaint is a 3D texture painter for iPad. Bake mesh maps from a high poly, paint PBR textures straight onto your model with Apple Pencil, and export them for Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot or glTF. Free to try on your own models; one purchase unlocks clean exports.

Long description

SlatePaint is a 3D texture painter for iPad, worked with touch and Apple Pencil. Import a mesh (OBJ, FBX, glTF, USD, PLY or STL), bake its maps from a high poly with a ray-traced baker and an automatic cage, and paint base color, roughness, metallic, height, normal, emissive, opacity and ambient occlusion on the model.

There are paint, fill, folder and filter layers, each with a blend mode per channel, and masks built from stacked effects. Generators read the baked maps to place edge wear, dirt and cavities, and Auto level fits each one to the model. The Shelf holds 71 smart materials in PBR, hand-painted and stylized looks, 53 scanned materials laid on at their real size, and 66 procedural grunges and patterns. Painted height shows as real displacement in the view.

Squeezing an Apple Pencil Pro opens a quick menu at its tip, and you feel shapes snap and picks land. Textures export with presets for Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot and glTF, or presets of your own with channel packing and formats from PNG to EXR and compressed DDS, plus a layered PSD per channel.

SlatePaint is free to download, and importing your own meshes, baking, painting and saving are free. Until the one in-app purchase, exported textures carry a faint “Made with SlatePaint” pattern; the purchase removes it and adds mesh export. There's no subscription and no account, and the app collects no data.

Key features

  • Painting in 3D, on the flat texture or both side by side, with pressure, tilt and barrel roll
  • Paint, fill, folder and filter layers; masks with generators, fill area, glow and drop shadow effects
  • Ray-traced baking of normal, world space normal, ambient occlusion, curvature, thickness, position, height and ID maps
  • 71 smart materials, 53 scanned materials (CC0), 30 smart masks, 66 grunges and patterns
  • UV, tri-planar, planar, cylindrical and spherical projections
  • Export presets for Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot and glTF; PNG, TGA, JPEG, TIFF, EXR and DDS; layered PSD
  • Quick menu on Apple Pencil Pro's squeeze, Pencil Pro haptics, glass or solid panels
  • Projects as single files in the Files app, carrying their high poly; Shortcuts to open a project, start from a sample or export again
